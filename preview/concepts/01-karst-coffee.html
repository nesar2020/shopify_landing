The roast, on the record

We publish the roast curve for every single lot.

Supermarket coffee hides behind "medium roast." We log every batch and print the curve on the bag — so you know exactly what happened between green bean and first crack.

01 — Freshness you can date

48 hours, roaster to doorstep

Coffee peaks 4–14 days after roasting. Ours arrives inside that window; shelf coffee is often 6+ months old before you open it.

02 — One lot, no blending

Traceable to a single farm

Blends exist to hide inconsistency. A single lot has nothing to hide — which is why we can print the producer's name and our price to them.

03 — Roasted to the lot

A curve built for each coffee

A dense 1,950 masl Gesha needs different heat than a natural Sidamo. Each lot gets its own profile, not a factory preset.

Questions

Before you commit.

What if I don't like a lot?

Tell us. We'll re-tune your profile and credit your next delivery. First bag ever? Covered by the second-cup guarantee — full refund, keep the coffee.

Can I pause around holidays?

Yes — skip, pause, or change frequency from your account in two taps, any time before the Sunday cut-off for that week's roast.

Whole bean or ground?

Both. If ground, tell us your brewer (V60, AeroPress, espresso, French press) and we grind to match on roast day.

What do producers actually get paid?

Our average green price last season was 2.1× the Fairtrade minimum. Each ticket prints the FOB price for that specific lot.
