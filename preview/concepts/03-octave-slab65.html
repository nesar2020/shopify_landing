The bump, up front

All the tactility before the travel

The 62 g peak sits at 0.5 mm — you feel the event, then glide. No mid-travel mush, no sandpaper. Consistency measured across 200 production switches: ±2.1 g.

Factory-lubed, honestly

Lubed like you would, not like factories do

Krytox 205g0 on rails and springs, applied by machine and audited by hand on 5% of every batch. Spring ping: not detectable above 28 dB room tone.

Hot-swap forever

Your board, your switches

5-pin hot-swap sockets rated for 100+ insertions. When you inevitably want to try something else, the Slab doesn't argue.

Why it sounds finished

Tuned at the factory, not on your desk mat.

Every trick the hobby invented — we just do them before shipping, with tooling instead of tweezers.

/01

Poron gasket sandwich

The plate floats between Poron strips top and bottom — a soft, even bounce and zero metal-on-metal contact anywhere in the case.

/02

Two-stage case fill

Bottom case lined with Poron over a sorbothane sheet. That's the deep "thock" everyone mods for — cast into the bill of materials instead.

/03

Force-break stabs

Screw-in stabilizers, wire-balanced and pre-clipped. Spacebar rattle is a manufacturing defect here, not a personality trait.

Can I use my own switches and keycaps?

Yes — 5-pin hot-swap sockets and standard Cherry-profile spacing. The included Tacton 62s and PBT caps are yours to keep either way.

What if I don't like tactile switches?

Why a 65% layout?
